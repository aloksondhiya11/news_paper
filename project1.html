INDIA WINS THE
WOULD CUP AFTER 28 YEARS!

Dhoni historic six seals
India's victory over Sri Lanka

MS Dhoni celebrates after hitting the winning six in the 2011 World Cup final.

MUMBAI, April 2, 2011

India created history by defeating Sri Lanka by six wickets in the final of the ICC Cricket World Cup 2011 at the Wankhede Stadium in Mumbai on Saturday.
Captain Mahendra Singh Dhoni led India to a memorable victory with an unbeaten 91 runs. Gautam Gambhir also played a brilliant innings of 97 runs, helping India chase the target of 275 runs.
Sri Lanka scored 274 runs for six wickets in 50 overs, with Mahela Jayawardene scoring an unbeaten century. India successfully chased the target in 48.2 overs.
Dhoni finished the match in style by hitting a six over long-on. The entire stadium erupted in celebration as India won its second ODI World Cup, 28 years after its first triumph in 1983.
The victory brought immense happiness to millions of Indian cricket fans. Sachin Tendulkar finally achieved his dream of winning the World Cup, as his teammates carried him on their shoulders in celebration.
